*[ARW]: Angle random walk: gyro white noise. Integrated angle uncertainty grows as ARW·√t; datasheets quote it in °/√h.
*[CEP]: Circular error probable: the radius of the circle, centred on the true position, that contains 50% of horizontal position errors.
*[ECEF]: Earth-centred, Earth-fixed frame (e): rotates with the Earth.
*[ECI]: Earth-centred inertial frame (i): does not rotate with the Earth.
*[GM]: First-order Gauss–Markov process: exponentially correlated random noise with a steady-state standard deviation and a correlation time.
*[GNSS]: Global navigation satellite system (GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou).
*[IMU]: Inertial measurement unit: three gyros and three accelerometers on orthogonal axes.
*[INS]: Inertial navigation system: an IMU plus the navigation equations that turn its outputs into position, velocity and attitude.
*[ISA]: International Standard Atmosphere: the reference profile of temperature and pressure with altitude.
*[MEMS]: Micro-electro-mechanical systems: low-cost silicon inertial sensors.
*[MSL]: Mean sea level.
*[NED]: North-East-Down: the local navigation frame (n) used throughout ins_sim.
*[QNH]: The altimeter pressure setting that makes a barometric altimeter read field elevation on the ground.
*[RLG]: Ring laser gyroscope: an optical gyro technology used in navigation-grade INS.
*[VRW]: Velocity random walk: accelerometer white noise. Integrated velocity uncertainty grows as VRW·√t; datasheets quote it in m/s/√h.
*[WGS-84]: World Geodetic System 1984: the reference ellipsoid, Earth rotation rate and gravity model used by ins_sim.
